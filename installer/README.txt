OPTCG Trainer for OPTCGSim — beta
==================================

INSTALL
1. Close OPTCGSim.
2. Double-click "Install OPTCG Trainer".
   (If Windows asks "Do you want to run this file?", press Run.)
3. It finds your OPTCGSim by itself. If you have more than one, pick the one you play with.
4. Open "OPTCGSim + Trainer" from your desktop. Use this icon from now on:
   the usual OPTCGSim still starts without the mod.
5. In the main menu press the blue OPTCG TRAINER button: your browser opens
   OPTCG Trainer, check the code is the same you see in the sim and press Link.
   You do it once.

IN A GAME
- Add note: write what you're thinking ("maybe I shouldn't have played X").
  You'll find it in the replay, at that exact moment.
- Focus mode: only the board, Back to Main and Concede, in full screen. Esc to exit.
- Camera icon (bottom right): saves a picture to share, with the board, both trashes,
  your deck bottom and the cards you know in your opponent's hand.
  It goes to Pictures > OPTCG Trainer.
- At the end of the game the replay is already on your account:
  "Open this game in OPTCG Trainer" opens it.

WHAT IT CHANGES
- In your OPTCGSim folder it adds one file: winhttp.dll. Nothing else is touched.
- Everything else is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OPTCGTrainer.
- It doesn't change the rules, the cards or the network of the game.
- It only talks to optcgtrainer.com, and only after you link your account.

UNINSTALL
Double-click "Uninstall OPTCG Trainer".
